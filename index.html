What Geniva makes

Pick one format, or make all four from the same brief.

Ebooks

An outline, chapters and a cover from your brief. Edit every page before you export.

Digital products

Templates, checklists, planners and guides you can sell as downloads.

Video

Short videos with a script, scenes and captions, for social posts or course intros.

AI visuals

Images and graphics that match the rest of your set, so everything looks like one brand.

Made for creators, coaches and small shops that sell digital downloads and want to publish more in less time.

How it works

  1. 1

    Write a brief

    Say what you want to make and who it's for, in a few lines.

  2. 2

    Review the drafts

    Geniva drafts each format. You compare them side by side.

  3. 3

    Edit and publish

    Change anything, export your files, then sell or share them where you like.

Building on Claude

We're building Geniva on Claude, Anthropic's AI model, through the Claude API. Claude writes the text: outlines, chapters, scripts and product copy. You decide what gets published.

Geniva is an independent project and is not affiliated with Anthropic.
