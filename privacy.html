Privacy policy

Last updated 10 October 2026

The short version

This website is a single static page. It has no accounts, no signup form, no cookies and no analytics. We don’t collect anything about you when you visit, apart from the technical records our web host keeps (see “Our web host” below).

If you email us

The Join early access button opens your own email app. If you send us a message, we receive your email address and whatever you write.

  • We use it to reply to you and to run early access.
  • Our email provider, Zoho Mail, stores and delivers our email.
  • We don’t sell your details or use them for advertising.

Our web host

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may record technical details such as your IP address and browser when you load a page. See GitHub’s privacy statement for how it handles that data.

Fonts

The fonts on this site are part of the page itself, so your browser doesn’t contact Google or any other font service.

Keeping and deleting your emails

We keep emails for as long as we need them to reply and manage early access. To have your emails deleted, write to onkar@geniva.me and ask.

Changes

If we add a signup form, analytics or cookies, we will update this page and its date before we start using them.

Contact

Questions about this policy: onkar@geniva.me.
